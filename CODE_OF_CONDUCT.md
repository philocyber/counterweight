# Code of Conduct

Counterweight follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions and any other channel the
maintainers run.

## In short

- Be respectful and assume good faith. Critique ideas and code, not people.
- No harassment, discrimination, sexualized language, personal attacks or publishing of anyone's
  private information.
- Keep it professional: this is a security project, and the people here are trying to help
  organizations defend themselves.

## Specific to this project

- Do not post real target data, captured traffic, credentials or details of systems you are not
  authorized to disclose. Use lab fixtures and redacted examples.
- Do not use project spaces to ask for or offer help attacking systems without authorization.
  See [Responsible use](README.md#responsible-use).
- Report vulnerabilities in Counterweight privately, as described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md),
  not in public issues.

## Reporting

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the maintainers through
[GitHub's private reporting for this repository](https://github.com/Philocyber/counterweight/security/advisories/new),
marking the report as a conduct issue. Reports are reviewed promptly and handled confidentially.
Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, issues and other contributions that
break this code, and may temporarily or permanently ban a contributor.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant, including its enforcement guidelines, is at the link
above and is incorporated here by reference.
